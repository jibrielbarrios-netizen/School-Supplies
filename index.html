Why This AI Matters to Our Community

🌍

Community Empowerment

Accessible computer vision turns everyday school laptops and phones into smart diagnostic tools—helping local classrooms, barangays, and youth clubs tackle real-world sorting, agricultural checks, safety awareness, and assistive identification without costly specialized hardware.

  • ✅ Zero-cost deployment via GitHub Pages
  • ✅ Works on standard classroom webcams
  • ✅ Exportable data for student research
🛡️

Student Privacy First

Unlike cloud AI apps that send student photos to remote servers, this scanner runs 100% locally in your web browser using TensorFlow.js. Every webcam frame and uploaded image stays strictly inside your device's memory and disappears when you close the tab.

Classroom Safe: Ideal for K–12 schools, science fairs, and public workshops where student data privacy is top priority.
🧠

How the AI Learns

  1. 1 Sample Gathering: Students capture labeled examples across distinct categories in Teachable Machine.
  2. 2 Transfer Learning: A lightweight MobileNet neural network learns visual patterns, shapes, and textures.
  3. 3 Instant Inference: Your browser computes probability scores in milliseconds with real-time visual feedback.
